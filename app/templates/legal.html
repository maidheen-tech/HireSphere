Your privacy matters

This policy explains how HireSphere handles information when you use this recruitment and applicant-tracking service. This portfolio application is a demonstration; do not submit sensitive personal information.

Information we collect

Account details such as your name, email, and password hash; profile details you provide; resumes and job applications; and basic service activity needed to operate the platform. Passwords are stored as salted hashes.

How information is used

We use information to provide job search, application, recruiter, and administrative functionality. Candidate profile information is shared with a recruiter when you apply to that recruiter's job. Uploaded resumes are restricted to their owner and recruiters handling the candidate's applications.

Your choices and security

You may update profile details, remove a resume, change your password, or deactivate your account. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can promise perfect security. In this demo, uploaded files and the SQLite database are stored on the host machine.
